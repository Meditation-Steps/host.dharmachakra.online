# The Flow of Devotion and the Glory of Kiirtana

## The Benefits of Kiirtana.

In the spiritual realm, *bhajanas* [devotional songs] and *kiirtana* [chanting the Lord’s name loudly] have great importance. While propagating *Dharma* [spiritual righteousness], they help in so many ways.

The devotional songs of Kabir and Nanak have spread the message of Dharma unto every house. The kiirtana of Caetanya Mahaprabhu and Tukaram awakened the spiritual feelings inherent in people’s minds, created collective sentiment, and expressed it all in the form of a great festival.

Devotional songs help in individual spiritual elevation. Similarly, kiirtana helps in the spiritual elevation of the collective life of human beings. Kiirtana cannot be done in solitude and silence. But devotional songs may be sung in seclusion and silence. Kiirtana is sung loudly and collectively. Kiirtana benefits the collectivity whereas devotional songs benefit the individual.

Kiirtana is sung to produce spiritual waves in others. As soon as others hear kiirtana, sweet spiritual feelings awaken within them. That is why we are bound to say, that in this crude, dogmatic and materialistic age, that it is a vital necessity to awaken the flow of spiritual feelings in the society through this kiirtana.

## Bábá Náma Kevalam

This *as‌t‌áks‌arii* [octo-syllabic] kiirtana mantra has been consecrated and infused with tremendous power. Any individual – no matter what alleged caste, religion, community, colour or country the person may claim to belong to – if the person sings kiirtana, he or she will be benefited. At the same time, society will also be benefited.

Kiirtana has the following benefits.

1) It is Sádhaná saháyakam, that is, it helps in performing spiritual practices like meditation. If after singing kiirtana, spiritual practice is done, spiritual aspirants truly progress in their meditation.
2) Adversities in collective life are removed. This is why it is said, Sámuhika bádhá vidúrakam.
3) If the *lalita mármika* dance [the dance which touches the core of the heart] is performed along with kiirtana, then benefits in the three spheres, physical, mental and spiritual, are obtained.

For instance,

- Vátrogvidurakam, that is, kiirtana heals gout and rheumatism.
- It enhances longevity. For this reason, kiirtana is said to be áyuvardhakam.
- It dispels worries, fears, doubts, etc. in the psychic sphere. So kiirtana is called cintavidurakam
- In the spiritual sphere it is Aánandadáyakam, that is, kiirtana bestows divine bliss.
